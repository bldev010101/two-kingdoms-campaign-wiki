# Carrion Crawler

**Formal family name:** House Voskarn  
**Slang name:** the Crawlers

House Voskarn did not build its first fortune beside fresh-planted orchards and ribbon-cuttings. Warehouses filled after armies dissolved; charters cheapened mid-plague; rebel lands forfeited faster than crows eat carrion. Scavenger wealth offends sentimental nobles—which is why sentimental nobles seldom notice Voskarn offers until bargains harden around them.

Crawlers rarely strike first sparks. They sift battlefields legally, patiently, politely—collecting debts of honor nobody still alive remembers pledging. Patient opportunists watch chaos like farmers watch weather: inconvenient for others, profitable if you remembered which silo leaked.

**Scavenger wealth:** they built their fortune after wars, plagues, bankruptcies, and failed rebellions.  
**Patient opportunists:** they rarely start conflicts, but they profit from every corpse left behind.
