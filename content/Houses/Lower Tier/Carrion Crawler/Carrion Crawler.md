# Carrion Crawler

**Formal family name:** House Voskarn  
**Slang name:** the Crawlers
**Kingdom:** [[Lycaria]]

House Carrion Crawler is a minor House on the trade route toward the north. It is known for scavenging the trade routes and performing repairs.

Recent rumors suggest that a garrison has been unduly held up in the House's holdings due to action taken by the Lord, who has a new mistress.
