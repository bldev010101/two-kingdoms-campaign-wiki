# Worg

**Formal family name:** House Raukden  
**Slang name:** the Greyfangs
**Kingdom:** [[Ballado]]

House Worg is a minor House in the plains of the Ballado Kingdom. It is aligned with [[Nightmare|House Nightmare]].

If the kingdoms go to war, House Worg plans to use its caravans to make extra money in the war-torn areas.
