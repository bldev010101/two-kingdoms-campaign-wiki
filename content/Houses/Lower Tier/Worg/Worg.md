# Worg

**Formal family name:** House Raukden  
**Slang name:** the Greyfangs

House Raukden counts coin humbly beside great houses—but counts kin without flinching. Pack loyalty means shared meat, shared watch, blood feuds prosecuted with choral discipline. Poor compared to dukes elsewhere, Greyfangs still field warbands that dissolve and reform like predators wheeling toward scent.

They bow to strength without pretending it disgusts them; exploit weakness without apologizing into poetry like wealthier villains do. Predatory pragmatism is reputation without varnish: alliances last while useful, grudges end when inconvenient, bargains favor whoever still has teeth. Nobles insult them casually; frontier captains seldom do twice.

**Pack loyalty:** they are poor compared to greater houses, but fiercely unified in war.  
**Predatory pragmatism:** they respect strength, exploit weakness, and do not pretend to be more noble than they are.
