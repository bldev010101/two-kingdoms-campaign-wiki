# Stirge

**Formal family name:** House Serrik  
**Slang name:** the Bloodwings

House Serrik learned early that pedigree can be leased. Marriage lines hook into richer houses like thin rivets binding heavy plate; favors compound into leverage; discreet loans ripen into quiet ownership of someone else’s crisis. Parasitic survival is how enemies describe it—and how Serrik describes patience with a predator’s grammar.

Taken one by one, a Serrik heir can look negligible: modest estates, middling armies, fortunes that shrug when accountants ask too loudly. Taken as a swarm—cousins clerking in rival chancelleries, minor branches married into debt, whisper-gatherings after midnight courts—they bleed dry what stronger houses thought was merely annoyance.

**Parasitic survival:** they attach themselves to stronger houses through marriages, loans, favors, and secrets.  
**Small but feared:** individually they seem unimpressive, but when they gather, they drain rivals dry.
