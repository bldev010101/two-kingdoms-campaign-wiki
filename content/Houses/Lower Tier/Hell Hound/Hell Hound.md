# Hell Hound

**Formal family name:** House Malgrave  
**Slang name:** the Ashhounds

House Malgrave rose on other people’s leashes—as household guards, punitive captains, debt-enforcement cavalry sworn to richer names. Fierce service bought them charters, petty baronies, the right to wear steel where soft houses wore silk. They understand loyalty contractually until loyalty becomes marrow.

Ashhounds harbor fires long after diplomats declare peace settled. Betrayals smolder beneath courtesy; heirs inherit grudges the way others inherit pastures. Burning grudges make Malgrave inconvenient allies—they remember slights sharper than treaties—and terrifying when someone breaks faith first.

**Fierce service:** they rose as loyal enforcers, bodyguards, or military retainers to a greater house.  
**Burning grudges:** they are infamous for pursuing betrayals long after wiser families would move on.
