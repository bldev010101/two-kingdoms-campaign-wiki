# Kruthik

**Formal family name:** House Threxen  
**Slang name:** the Ironbrood

House Threxen treats family like infantry treats shield walls—personal glory matters less than the line holding. Cadet branches swarm practical trades: smithing, masonry, caravan escort, garrison rotations. Hive discipline breeds dull ballrooms but durable sieges; Threxens argue in meeting halls and obey on the battlefield with unsettling synchronicity.

They lack romance in song and glitter in tapestry. Ironbrood strength is uglier—burrowed leases, fortified farms, heirs raised to replace losses without melodrama. Armored persistence means uprooting them requires expense greater than embarrassment: dig them out stone by stone or learn to treaty around them permanently.

**Hive discipline:** the family values obedience, reproduction, expansion, and family unity above personal glory.  
**Armored persistence:** they are not glamorous, but they are very hard to uproot once established.
