# Roper

**Formal family name:** House Granthor
**Slang name:** the Graspers

[[Roper|House Roper]] has a minor mine of [[Gleaming magics]] and [[Glittering magics]]. Rumor has it that the mine was recently taken over by a [[Dragon|dragon]], and the [[Dwarves|dwarves]] of House Roper were ousted.

One member of the house is [[Brunthor Granthor]]. He was forced to sell his [[Avalanche Hammer of Smashing]] to pay his family's debt because the mine is out of operation.
