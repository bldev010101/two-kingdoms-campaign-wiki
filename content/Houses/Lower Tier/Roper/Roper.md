# Roper

**Formal family name:** House Stonemire  
**Slang name:** the Graspers

House Stonemire traps with patience that feels like stonework curing. Toll clauses tighten like twine; debts spider through collateral nobody valued until called; soldiers appear at crossings marked legal on parchment nobody read. Trap-setters wield contract law and ambush rumor with identical calm—they prefer enemies convinced they walked themselves into it.

Witnesses swear Stonemire lords scarcely twitch for years—no flashy wars, few glittering fairs—then fortunes snap closed like trapjaws underground. Stillness as strength terrifies counselors who mistake quiet for stupidity and wake to find heirs married, roads tolled, and rivals tangled beyond appeal.

**Trap-setters:** their power comes from debts, contracts, tolls, ambushes, and legal snares.  
**Stillness as strength:** they wait years before acting, then pull enemies into ruin all at once.
