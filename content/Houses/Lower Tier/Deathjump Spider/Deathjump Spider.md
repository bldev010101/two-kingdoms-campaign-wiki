# Deathjump Spider

**Formal family name:** House Arvess  
**Slang name:** the Jumpers

House Arvess excels at momentum nobody scheduled. Surprise raids unravel supply chains; challenged duels end before seconds finish counting; hostage exchanges twist into alliances before heralds synchronize lies. Ambush tradition is not mere bandit swagger—it is choreography trained on kitchen tables as much as parade grounds.

Arvess kin, fosterlings, oath-bound cooks, caravan runners, minor cousins sworn to reciprocal silence—all knot into obligation webs outsiders misread until tugged wrong. Webbed loyalties make Arvess useful as blades and irritating as diplomats: favors travel sideways faster than hierarchies pretend.

**Ambush tradition:** they specialize in sudden raids, duels, kidnappings, and surprise alliances.  
**Webbed loyalties:** even their minor cousins and servants are tied into a dense network of obligations.
