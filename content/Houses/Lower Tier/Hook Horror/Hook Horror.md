# Hook Horror

**Formal family name:** House Korravik  
**Slang name:** the Cavehooks

House Korravik’s old seats cling to bleak stone the way claws cling to wet granite—narrow approaches, baffling forks, ridges that shrug off ladders. Hidden brutality is geography made doctrine: armies learn quickly that occupying Korravik land costs more boots than morale allows. Outsiders curse the bleakness; Korravik calls it architecture.

Echo-watchers is more than folklore. Cousins seeded through mining towns hear couriers cough two valleys over; relays of runners carry scraps of rumor from cavern markets to manor hearths without paper trails. Listening from the dark is how a minor house survives when sunlit heraldry outweighs theirs.

**Hidden brutality:** their old holdings are remote, rocky, and difficult to invade.  
**Echo-watchers:** they are excellent informants because their family history is built on listening from the dark.
