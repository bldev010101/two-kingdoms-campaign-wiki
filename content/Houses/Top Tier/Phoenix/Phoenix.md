# Phoenix

**Formal family name:** House Aurelian  
**Slang name:** the Ashwings

House Aurelian treats flame as pedigree. Their chronicles read less like uninterrupted triumph than like disciplined resurrection: coups survived, castles lost, charters stripped, heirs scattered—and still the line returned, often sharper than before. Practical folk call it ruthless reinvention; Aurelians frequently call it design.

Sacred pride steers their diplomacy. Aurelian envoys seldom argue legitimacy with common mercantile logic; they speak as heirs to precedent, oath, lineage, sometimes divinity itself. Outsiders resent the presumption until they watch the house claw back scraps of power and weld them into thrones again. The Ashwing name is slang for rumor and resurgence: ashes are not failure to them—they are camouflage until the wings spread.

**Reborn from ruin:** the family has been overthrown, exiled, or burned out before and always returned.  
**Sacred pride:** they believe their bloodline has a divine or historical right to rule.
