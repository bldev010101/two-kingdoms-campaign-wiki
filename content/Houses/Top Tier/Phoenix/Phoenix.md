# Phoenix

**Formal family name:** House Aurelian  
**Slang name:** the Ashwings
**Kingdom:** [[Lycaria]]

House Phoenix is allied with [[Sphinx|House Sphinx]] and [[Umber Hulk|House Umber Hulk]].

House Phoenix does not have the same economic power as some other Top Tier Houses, but it has consistently maintained substantial political power within the [[Symposium]]. Lord Caelian Aurelian, Lady Mirelle Aurelian, and Lord Oren Aurelian currently hold three seats on the 12-seat Symposium, giving the House a larger share than most other Houses.

House Phoenix relies on House Sphinx's deep understanding of the religious aspects of the kingdoms to help maintain its political power. House Phoenix is skilled in diplomacy but does not have the same expertise in religion.

The alliance could be weakened if House Phoenix gained another source of religious knowledge or if House Sphinx's religious expertise became less important to Phoenix's politicians.

House Umber Hulk is governed by the Deep Council, led by First Councillor Hadrik Karthum. An aboleth named Ulu-Vor beneath that House's castle influences the council through dreams and maintains its alliance with House Phoenix. Killing Ulu-Vor or exposing its influence would allow House Umber Hulk to reconsider the alliance.
