# Sphinx

**Formal family name:** House Veyrath  
**Slang name:** the Riddle-Lords

House Veyrath keeps civilization’s fragile paperwork the way dragons keep hoards—with teeth you only notice when someone reaches carelessly. Genealogical tables scratched with marginalia, charters whose clauses braid across centuries, prophecy collections filed beside tax disputes, treaties whose true meaning wakes only under stress: theirs is a vault of riddles disguised as records.

Courtiers dislike Veyrath answers because the answers seldom arrive as answers. Silence, counter-questions, riddles mirrored back, and immaculate courtesy make them prized envoys—and unnerving spouses in alliance. Proudly inscrutable is polite speech for dangerous: you never know whether you negotiated with a scholar, a sphinx, or a trap.

**Keeper of secrets:** they preserve ancient contracts, genealogies, prophecies, and forbidden truths.  
**Proudly inscrutable:** they rarely answer directly, making them feared diplomats and dangerous allies.
