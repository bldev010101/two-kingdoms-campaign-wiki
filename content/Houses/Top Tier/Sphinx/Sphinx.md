# Sphinx

**Formal family name:** House Veyrath  
**Slang name:** the Riddle-Lords
**Kingdom:** [[Lycaria]]

House Sphinx has an allegiance with [[Chimera|House Chimera]], [[Displacer Beast|House Displacer Beast]], and [[Phoenix|House Phoenix]]. Its support is helping House Displacer Beast remain afloat after that House lost access to its mines.

High Councillor Ilyra Veyrath chairs House Sphinx's ruling council.

House Sphinx has a deep understanding of the religious aspects of the kingdoms. The Tridecagon stands in the middle of its lands. There are thirteen major religions within the kingdoms, and although they do not often interact, rumors suggest that their religious heads meet within the Tridecagon for an unknown purpose.

House Phoenix relies on House Sphinx's religious knowledge to help maintain its political power within the [[Symposium]].

Tavian Veyrath of House Sphinx and Elowen Trivayne of House Chimera are held hostage by the Oracle Hags Mora, Cethra, and Veska within a fey court. The Hags have prophesied that the hostages will die if the alliance between the Houses is broken. Rescuing the hostages or defeating the Oracle Hags would remove the threat maintaining the alliance.

House Sphinx once had a major stake in the Water Residum Mine in [[Osperath]], until [[Ballado|House Ballado]] took over all handling of the mine about a generation ago.

The current generation of House Sphinx has used its deep influence with the other [[Houses]] to maintain its High Tier status. Some say the House is losing its grip without a true mine under its control.

Its holdings are on the western outskirts of Osperath. The area has become increasingly cold over the last century, adding to the difficulties of living there in the future.
