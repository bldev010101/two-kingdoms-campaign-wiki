# Gorgon

**Formal family name:** House Dravosk  
**Slang name:** the Iron Bulls
**Kingdom:** [[Ballado]]

House Gorgon is allied with [[Roc|House Roc]], [[Tarrasque|House Tarrasque]], and [[Manticore|House Manticore]].

Lord Damar Dravosk leads House Gorgon.

Seraphine Dravosk, daughter of Lord Damar Dravosk, is betrothed to Corven Kyclyan, son of House Roc's Lord Aeric Kyclyan. She is in love with Prince Lucan Malrec of [[Manticore|House Manticore]].

Queen Velyra Vaskyr of [[Wyvern|House Wyvern]] is in love with Halvek Dravosk, one of Lord Damar Dravosk's sons, and would rather ally her House with House Gorgon than House Roc.

House Manticore possesses an Ancestral War Helm containing the spirit of General Corveth Malrec, who compels House Manticore to honor its alliance with House Gorgon. Defeating the spirit or destroying the War Helm would break its control over the alliance.

House Gorgon's alliance with House Tarrasque is maintained by four elder patriarchs: Garran and Othric Dravosk of House Gorgon, and Merek and Torvan Morcant of House Tarrasque. They have known one another for a very long time and remain closely tied. The rest of both families dislike them, regard them as strange, and suspect that they suffer from dementia. They also hold strange opinions.

Removing the four patriarchs from influence would break the alliance. They could be killed, placed in care, or sent away somewhere remote.
