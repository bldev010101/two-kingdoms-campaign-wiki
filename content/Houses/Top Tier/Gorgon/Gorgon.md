# Gorgon

**Formal family name:** House Dravosk  
**Slang name:** the Iron Bulls
**Kingdom:** [[Ballado]]

House Dravosk built its legend on hoofbeats and heraldry that looks less like flourish and more like regulation. Heavy cavalry schools, punitive tax enforcement with military precision, and court law delivered as marching orders—all of it braided into identity. Outsiders caricature Dravosk as brute strength; insiders know the brute part is disciplined mass, timed charges, formations that punish hesitation.

Their authority bites when it freezes. Judicial pronouncements from Dravosk benches—or from Dravosk-backed crowns—carry a reputation slower to forgive than armies are to dissolve. Rivals whisper of petrifying authority: condemnation as a siege without walls. Crossing the Iron Bulls is less a duel than a geology lesson in how reputations bury themselves.

**Implacable discipline:** their family history is built on military order, heavy cavalry, and harsh law.  
**Petrifying authority:** rivals fear their judgments because once House Dravosk condemns someone, they rarely relent.
