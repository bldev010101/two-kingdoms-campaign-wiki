# Roc

**Formal family name:** House Kyclyan  
**Slang name:** the Stormwings
**Kingdom:** [[Ballado]]

House Roc is a major House under the Ballado Kingdom. It provides air support with zeppelins, flying kites, and a few Pegasus riders.

Lord Aeric Kyclyan leads House Roc.

House Roc is allied with [[Gorgon|House Gorgon]], [[Hydra|House Hydra]], [[Nightmare|House Nightmare]], and [[Wyvern|House Wyvern]].

House Roc's alliance with House Gorgon is supported by the betrothal of Seraphine Dravosk, daughter of Lord Damar Dravosk, to Corven Kyclyan, son of Lord Aeric Kyclyan. House Roc purchases a large amount of House Hydra's healing magic, provides a market for it throughout the land, and delivers House Hydra's goods using its airships and air cavalry.

House Nightmare supplies Ballado's horseback cavalry and relies on House Roc's air cavalry. Effective anti-air magic would reduce that dependence.

Queen Velyra Vaskyr of House Wyvern would rather leave her alliance with House Roc for House Gorgon, but her main political adviser, Chancellor Orren Vale, has been seduced by the succubus Velisara and refuses to allow the change.

The House is based on the western edge of [[Osperath]] and sends some of its dwarves into the Water Magic Mine in Osperath.
