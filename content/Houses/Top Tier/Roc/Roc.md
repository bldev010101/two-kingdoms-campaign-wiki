# Roc

**Formal family name:** House Caerwyn  
**Slang name:** the Stormwings

House Caerwyn rules in places where ladders break and maps become lies: cliff cities strung across air, fortress roads braided along ridges, alpine passes taxed like rivers, watchposts that stare down cloudbanks. Visitors speak of grandeur; locals speak of weather. Dominion this high trains a certain arrogance tempered by practicality—supply lines are theology up here.

Stormwings tolerate independence below—as long as it does not touch their sky. Borders drawn along sheer rock are metaphors sharpened into law. Intruders raid lowland farms; threats to cliffs, ropes, nests of signal-beacons, or the narrow routes that bind regions together summon disproportionate retaliation. Territorial wrath is not spite; it is aerodynamics taught with spearheads.

**High dominion:** they rule from mountain fortresses, sky roads, cliff cities, or highland passes.  
**Territorial wrath:** they tolerate independence below them, but punish anyone who threatens their heights or borders.
