# Hydra

**Formal family name:** House Kaelroth  
**Slang name:** the Many-Heads
**Kingdom:** [[Ballado]]

House Hydra is allied with [[Roc|House Roc]], [[Tarrasque|House Tarrasque]], and [[Basilisk|House Basilisk]].

Lady Ysara Kaelroth leads House Hydra.

House Roc purchases a large amount of House Hydra's healing magic, provides a market for it throughout the land, and delivers House Hydra's goods using its airships and air cavalry. Finding another way to deliver the goods could change the alliance between the Houses.

Lady Ysara Kaelroth holds substantial blackmail against Marshal-Lord Edric Morcant and Chancellor Beren Morcant of House Tarrasque. Removing or stopping the blackmail could break the alliance. House Hydra is already ostracized and viewed as strange, and making it fully disreputable could push it out completely.

Lady Sabine Serevan, wife of House Basilisk's Lord Orsian Serevan, is being slowly turned to stone by a cursed Petrifying Eye. House Hydra's healing magic slows the petrification, maintaining House Basilisk's dependence on House Hydra. Destroying the Eye or finding another cure would break that dependence.

House Hydra has suddenly become isolationist. They operate a mine that produces Healing magics.

A team from House Hydra is in [[Avriel]] for the [[Blood Dice]] event, but its members are not talkative.

[[Princess Minerva Ballado]] has said that House Hydra has been acting strangely recently.
