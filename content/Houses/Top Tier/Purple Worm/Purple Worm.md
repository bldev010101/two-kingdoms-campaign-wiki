# Purple Worm

**Formal family name:** House Odaku
**Slang name:** the Deep Worms
**Kingdom:** [[Lycaria]]

Mine-Matriarch Nara Odaku leads House Purple Worm.

House Purple Worm operates elemental mines, including mines that produce Lightning magic. The kingdoms rely on these mines for elemental magic, giving House Purple Worm a strong economic position.

House Purple Worm has no ties to the strange events and changes occurring within the kingdoms and is among the Houses least likely to want war. It has more to lose if war damages the kingdoms' economic stability. The players are already aligned with the House.

If dangerous alliances must be shifted, House Purple Worm could provide a more stable alternative because it has strong economic reasons to preserve the status quo. If it is pulled into the war, it becomes the last domino to fall on the Lycarian side.

A temple was found underneath the House sewers near these mines.

One completed [[Oak and Bone Relics|Oak and Bone Relic]] was used to open the temple.
