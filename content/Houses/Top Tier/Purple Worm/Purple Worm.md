# Purple Worm

**Formal family name:** House Odaku
**Slang name:** the Deep Worms
**Kingdom:** [[Lycaria]]

House Odaku’s strength is seldom visible from a palace balcony. Mines, shafts, drowned galleries, reinforced smuggling tunnels, and buried arterial roads knot beneath territories they steward—or discreetly annex through debt instruments that read like geology. Aboveground they may wear modest court dress; underneath, iron and coin move on schedules outsiders never map.

Their expansion is ingestion: failing baronies become leaseholds; ruined houses surrender mineral rights before they notice the fine print; banks grow friendly when tunnels connect vaults Odaku already influences. Critics call them devouring ambition; admirers say they understand that power is strata. Storms punish the sky; worms endure the grind.

**Subterranean wealth:** their power comes from mines, tunnels, buried roads, or hidden vaults.  
**Devouring ambition:** they absorb weaker houses, lands, and debts until they control everything beneath the surface.
