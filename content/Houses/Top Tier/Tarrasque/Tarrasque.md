# Tarrasque

**Formal family name:** House Morcant  
**Slang name:** the Worldbreakers
**Kingdom:** [[Ballado]]

House Tarrasque is aligned with [[Nightmare|House Nightmare]] and allied with [[Gorgon|House Gorgon]] and [[Hydra|House Hydra]].

Marshal-Lord Edric Morcant leads House Tarrasque. Chancellor Beren Morcant is another of its top lords.

Lady Ysara Kaelroth of House Hydra holds substantial blackmail against Marshal-Lord Edric Morcant and Chancellor Beren Morcant. Removing or stopping that blackmail could break the alliance between the Houses.

House Tarrasque's alliance with [[Gorgon|House Gorgon]] is maintained by four elder patriarchs: Merek and Torvan Morcant of House Tarrasque, and Garran and Othric Dravosk of House Gorgon. They have known one another for a very long time and remain closely tied. The rest of both families dislike them, regard them as strange, and suspect that they suffer from dementia. They also hold strange opinions.

Removing the four patriarchs from influence would break the alliance. They could be killed, placed in care, or sent away somewhere remote.

House Tarrasque represents the main army of [[Ballado]]. If it enters the war, the conflict becomes a kingdom-wide war between Ballado and [[Lycaria]].
