# Tarrasque

**Formal family name:** House Morcant  
**Slang name:** the Worldbreakers
**Kingdom:** [[Lycaria]]

House Morcant’s sigil beast is spoken of in battlefield sermons and treaty halls alike: slow to rise, impossible to divert, and oddly patient for something with a reputation for devastation. Historically, Morcant armies did not win every skirmish; they won endurance. Their ancestors repeatedly ended continental wars simply by refusing to collapse while rivals burned fuel, fortunes, and legitimacy.

For stretches of centuries the house can lie almost dormant—investments quietly compounding, marriage lines quietly knitting, heirs raised out of spectacle. Outsiders mistake the silence for weakness. Past ages show the opposite pattern: once Morcant commits, rumor outruns riders. Neighboring realms scramble couriers not because victory is unclear, but because delay might mean there is nothing left worth contesting.

**Unstoppable:** their ancestors ended wars by simply outlasting every rival.  
**Dormant menace:** they may stay quiet for generations, but when they move, kingdoms panic.
