# Displacer Beast

**Formal family name:** House Velkyr  
**Slang name:** the Shifters
**Kingdom:** [[Lycaria]]

House Displacer Beast is allied with [[Sphinx|House Sphinx]] and [[Bulette|House Bulette]]. It lost access to its mines long ago, and support from House Sphinx is helping it remain afloat and retain its Top Tier position.

Lord Vyaharian is too old to realize that his House is falling and would never consider switching kingdoms. His son, Loeket, believes that switching kingdoms would be better.

If House Displacer Beast could find an ally in [[Ballado]], it might be able to preserve its position without House Sphinx and break the alliance.

House Bulette has also helped House Displacer Beast remain afloat through underhanded black-market deals. House Bulette is now looking for another partner because it sees that House Displacer Beast is falling from its position.
