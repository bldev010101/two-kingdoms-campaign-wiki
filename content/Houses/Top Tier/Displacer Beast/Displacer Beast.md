# Displacer Beast

**Formal family name:** House Velkyr  
**Slang name:** the Shifters

House Velkyr has survived eras that swallowed louder houses by dancing where watchers expect them to stand. False heirs circulate as sanctioned rumor; estates vanish behind shell companies nested like shields; diplomats speak fluent doublespeak salted with sincerity just believable enough to buy time. To hunt a Velkyr scandal is to watch it slide two steps sideways and become someone else’s problem.

Their public face is etiquette with predatory grooming: salons, fashions, duelists who duel with apologies first. Beneath manners lies a pedigree of disappeared rivals—never proven, seldom forgotten. Elegant predation is not melodrama here; it is craft. Outsiders underestimate how often grace is camouflage until the ballroom empties early.

**Elusive survival:** their house survives through misdirection, false heirs, hidden estates, and political doublespeak.  
**Elegant predation:** they appear refined and graceful, but their history is full of assassinations and vanished rivals.
