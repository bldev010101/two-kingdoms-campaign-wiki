# Nightmare

**Formal family name:** House Vaelmoor  
**Slang name:** the Blackmanes
**Kingdom:** [[Ballado]]

House Nightmare is a proud house of cavalry and magic users. It has agreed to help [[Owlbear|House Owlbear]] if needed.

Ranger-Marshal Maelin Vaelmoor leads House Nightmare.

House Nightmare is aligned with [[Tarrasque|House Tarrasque]], [[Worg|House Worg]], and [[Roc|House Roc]]. Its members are the horseback rangers of the North and supply horseback cavalry for [[Ballado]], while House Roc supplies air cavalry. They would be the closest units to be pulled into a conflict if House Roc went truly to war.

House Nightmare relies on House Roc's air cavalry but does not want to remain aligned with it. House Nightmare's members are good people, and House Roc has been acting strangely.

If House Nightmare gains effective anti-air magic, such as fireball or flame magic, it would no longer depend as heavily on House Roc. House Nightmare has been working with [[Owlbear|House Owlbear]], which may have magic that could help.

[[Solomon David]] arranged this agreement for House Owlbear.
