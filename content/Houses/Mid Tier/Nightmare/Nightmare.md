# Nightmare

**Formal family name:** House Vaelmoor  
**Slang name:** the Blackmanes

House Vaelmoor’s ascent refuses tidy moral chapter breaks. Chroniclers argue whether their rise leaned on brilliance, massacre, rumored pacts sworn in chapel cellars—or all three braided into dusk-colored prestige. Dark prestige hangs on them like incense: undeniable influence, undeniable unease at how precisely it accumulated.

Against outsiders they can be brittle and theatrical; among themselves Blackmanes keep memory like arsenic—measured doses across lifetimes. Insults lodge; enemies discover consequences years later, routed through heirs who never personally heard the slight. Haunting loyalty is family policy wearing a funeral veil.

**Dark prestige:** their rise came through war, fear, or a bargain no one speaks of openly.  
**Haunting loyalty:** they remember every insult and pursue old enemies across generations.
