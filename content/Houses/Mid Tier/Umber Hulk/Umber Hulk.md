# Umber Hulk

**Formal family name:** House Karthum  
**Slang name:** the Deep Hulks

House Karthum keeps its weight below eye level until it matters: ore rights that quietly knot whole cantons; fortress-keeps drilled into hillside; prisons whose wardens swear odd oaths of mutual silence; caravan routes that reroute underground when winters bite. Buried strength is not secrecy for sport—it is geology they trust more than heraldry.

On the surface Karthums speak plainly, blunt as locked gates. Beneath that voice, fortunes shift via contracts filed in strata few clerks revisit. Outsiders conclude they are simple; insiders learn prediction errors after the fact. Deep Hulk politics confuse because the obvious move is seldom the loaded one—until it is obvious all at once.

**Buried strength:** their power comes from mines, fortresses, prisons, and underground roads.  
**Confounding presence:** their politics are blunt on the surface but strangely difficult to predict.
