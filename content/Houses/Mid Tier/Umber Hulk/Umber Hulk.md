# Umber Hulk

**Formal family name:** House Karthum  
**Slang name:** the Deep Hulks
**Kingdom:** [[Lycaria]]

House Umber Hulk is allied with [[Phoenix|House Phoenix]].

House Umber Hulk is governed by the Deep Council, led by First Councillor Hadrik Karthum.

An aboleth named Ulu-Vor beneath House Umber Hulk's castle influences the Deep Council through dreams and maintains its alliance with House Phoenix.

Killing Ulu-Vor or exposing its influence would allow House Umber Hulk to reconsider the alliance.
