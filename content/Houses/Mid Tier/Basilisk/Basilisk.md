# Basilisk

**Formal family name:** House Serevan  
**Slang name:** the Stone-Eyes

House Serevan rose through judgment like others rise through armies. Tribunals wore their colors; inquiry chambers borrowed their librarians; executions carried seals that traced back to ancestral desks. Fear of judgment is quieter than fear of siege—it follows you indoors. Serevan built a name where law becomes theater and theater becomes precedent.

Gossip treats their attention like weather you cannot outrun: a Stone-Eye lord pausing at your name invites rumors of ruin whether anything happens next or not. Scandal blossoms in their wake; fortunes freeze mid-transaction; suicides appear in penny broadsheets with suspicious neatness. Cursed gaze is superstition to scholars and strategy to rivals.

**Feared judgment:** the family built its name as magistrates, inquisitors, or executioners.  
**Cursed gaze:** people believe their attention brings ruin, scandal, or death.
