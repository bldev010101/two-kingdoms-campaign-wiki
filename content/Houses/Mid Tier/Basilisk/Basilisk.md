# Basilisk

**Formal family name:** House Serevan  
**Slang name:** the Stone-Eyes
**Kingdom:** [[Ballado]]

House Basilisk is allied with [[Hydra|House Hydra]].

Lord Orsian Serevan leads House Basilisk. His wife, Lady Sabine Serevan, is being slowly turned to stone by a cursed Petrifying Eye. House Hydra's healing magic slows the petrification, maintaining the alliance.

Destroying the Petrifying Eye or finding another cure would remove House Basilisk's dependence on House Hydra.
