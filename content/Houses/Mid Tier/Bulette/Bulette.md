# Bulette

**Formal family name:** House Durnholt  
**Slang name:** the Landsharks
**Kingdom:** [[Lycaria]]

House Bulette is allied with [[Displacer Beast|House Displacer Beast]].

Guildmistress Daska Durnholt leads House Bulette.

House Bulette functions more like a thieves' guild and is known for underhanded black-market dealings. It has helped House Displacer Beast remain afloat through black-market deals.

House Bulette now sees that House Displacer Beast is falling from its position and is looking for another partner. It would leave the alliance if it found another House willing to participate in its underhanded and thieving activities.
