# Bulette

**Formal family name:** House Durnholt  
**Slang name:** the Landsharks

House Durnholt favors conclusions over conversation. Negotiations shorten when scouts report Durnholts marching; diplomats learn to distinguish bluff from the moment the earth stops politely pretending calm. Sudden violence is doctrine dressed as folklore—appearing where maps said empty, punching through weakened lines before heralds agree on grammar.

Their land thirst is ancestral paperwork sharpened into spearheads: grants argued from dust, boundaries inked generations before current crops, pastures claimed because a grandfather once rode there in rain. Challengers invoke equity; Landsharks invoke lineage and mean it bitterly enough to fight over furrows nobody else remembered.

**Sudden violence:** they are known for decisive attacks that seem to come from nowhere.  
**Territorial greed:** their old land claims are aggressive, ancient, and bitterly enforced.
