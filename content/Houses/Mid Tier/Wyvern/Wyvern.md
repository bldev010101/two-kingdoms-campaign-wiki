# Wyvern

**Formal family name:** House Vaskyr  
**Slang name:** the Venomwings
**Kingdom:** [[Ballado]]

House Wyvern is allied with [[Roc|House Roc]].

Queen Velyra Vaskyr is the widowed queen of House Wyvern.

Queen Velyra would rather be allied with [[Gorgon|House Gorgon]] because she is in love with Halvek Dravosk, one of Lord Damar Dravosk's sons.

Chancellor Orren Vale, House Wyvern's main political adviser, has been seduced by the succubus Velisara and refuses to allow the queen to switch alliances. Removing Velisara's influence could allow Queen Velyra to move House Wyvern's alliance from House Roc to House Gorgon.
