# Wyvern

**Formal family name:** House Vaskyr  
**Slang name:** the Venomwings

House Vaskyr made its chronicles where borders fray: lightning raids, hawk-eyed scouts, siege towers barged uphill, and doctrines written in ash on frontier stone. Their campaigns favor speed—arrive, sting, spoil the supply chain, vanish along ridge routes no tax map bothers to sketch. Outsiders underestimate Wyvern contempt for fair battle; insiders call it realism.

Venomic pride has an edge shaped by resentment. Dragon-titled great houses claim ancient sky-right and divine precedent; Wyvern heirs memorize that slight with each generation. Proof becomes obsession: bolder escapades, glittering armor, conspicuous valor in wars they were not summoned to fight. Proud but lesser-born is the insult they endure and the fuel they ride.

**Border raiders:** their history is full of fast strikes, aerial assaults, and hard frontier warfare.  
**Proud but lesser-born:** they resent the dragon-associated great houses and constantly try to prove themselves.
