# Owlbear

**Formal family name:** House Brackenmere  
**Slang name:** the Clawbeaks

House Brackenmere carries noble blood roughed by weather. Their heirs learn trees like others learn heraldry—sap-stained hands, hunts that double as surveying, lodges where charters are scratched clean of court perfume. Wild nobility is not an insult among them; it is pedigree that refuses varnish. Palaces bore Brackenmeres faster than famine does.

Alliance with Brackenmere demands patience—they forget RSVPs they consider trivial, bristle at condescension, bargain like bargainers who might walk away forever. Threaten their kin or encroach on ancestral thickets, though, and the house answers with furious economy. Protective ferocity earns them unreliable friends and formidable neighbors.

**Wild nobility:** they are old-blooded but rough, more comfortable in forests and hunting lodges than court.  
**Protective ferocity:** they are difficult allies, but terrifying when defending kin or land.
