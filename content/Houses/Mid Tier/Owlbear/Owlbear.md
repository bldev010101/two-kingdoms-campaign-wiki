# Owlbear

**Formal family name:** House Brackenmere  
**Slang name:** the Clawbeaks
**Kingdom:** [[Lycaria]]

House Owlbear is allied with [[Chimera|House Chimera]]. It is a nomadic house that wanders the plains and light forests in the northwest of the kingdom.

[[Solomon David]] arranged for [[Nightmare|House Nightmare]] to help House Owlbear if needed. House Owlbear has said that Solomon is welcome to take a high position in the temple of the [[Inexplicable Machinations of the Inner Soul]].

[[Jakaya]], a dull purple Dragonborn paladin, is allied with House Owlbear.
