# Manticore

**Formal family name:** House Malrec  
**Slang name:** the Barbtails

House Malrec has polished the art of the civilized blade. Reception lines run on clockwork etiquette; apologies arrive before challenges; favors are draped in language lovely enough that recipients sign before they inspect the hinges. Poisoned courtesy is barely metaphor in some seasons—Malrec thrives when politics resembles perfumed hunting.

Older stories linger like smoke: enemies stripped of honor before estates, heirs disgraced in verse before forfeiture, treaties that read as kindness until the laughter stops. Critics call Malrec gratuitous; Malrec insists humiliation is instruction. Either way the Barbtail reputation warns courtiers that a smile can still carry Barb.

**Poisoned courtesy:** they are famous for elegant manners and lethal politics.  
**Cruel reputation:** old stories say they prefer enemies humiliated before they are destroyed.
