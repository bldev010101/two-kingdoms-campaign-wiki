# Manticore

**Formal family name:** House Malrec  
**Slang name:** the Barbtails
**Kingdom:** [[Ballado]]

House Manticore is allied with [[Gorgon|House Gorgon]].

Lady Ysilde Malrec leads House Manticore.

House Manticore possesses an Ancestral War Helm containing the spirit of General Corveth Malrec, an ancestor who compels the House to honor its alliance with House Gorgon. Defeating the spirit or destroying the War Helm would break its control over the alliance.

[[Dahliala Sharp]], a participant in the [[Blood Sports]], is pledged to House Manticore.

Prince Lucan Malrec is loved by Seraphine Dravosk, daughter of [[Gorgon|House Gorgon's]] Lord Damar Dravosk, who is betrothed to Corven Kyclyan, son of [[Roc|House Roc]]'s Lord Aeric Kyclyan.
