# Chimera

**Formal family name:** House Trivayne  
**Slang name:** the Threeborn

House Trivayne does not boast a single clean origin myth. Their line was stitched together from rival blood—by treaty marriage, forced conquest, careful adoption, and the occasional scandal sealed with ink instead of steel. Coats of arms in Trivayne halls show subtle mismatches: colors that argue, crests that share a shield without quite agreeing. Outsiders call them patchwork nobility; insiders call that adaptability.

The Threeborn name hints at triads: three founding claims, three ruling factions, three tempers that never quite merge. Trivayne adapts fast—new tactics, new alliances, new rhetoric—yet the same inheritance that makes them formidable also primes them for sudden schisms. A family forged from enemies sometimes remembers how the forging felt.

**Mixed inheritance:** the family was formed by marriage, conquest, or adoption between rival bloodlines.  
**Volatile ambition:** they are powerful and adaptable, but prone to internal conflict.
