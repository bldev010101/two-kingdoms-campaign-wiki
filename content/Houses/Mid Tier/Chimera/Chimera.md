# Chimera

**Formal family name:** House Trivayne  
**Slang name:** the Threeborn
**Kingdom:** [[Lycaria]]

House Chimera is allied with [[Owlbear|House Owlbear]] and has an allegiance with [[Sphinx|House Sphinx]]. It rules the city of [[Ferndale]] and operates a mine of Charm magic within the Castle of Ferndale.

Lord Cassian Trivayne leads House Chimera.

House Chimera withdrew the garrison that protected House Owlbear, leaving House Owlbear upset and open to another alliance if a different House provides a garrison.

Elowen Trivayne of House Chimera and Tavian Veyrath of House Sphinx are held hostage by the Oracle Hags Mora, Cethra, and Veska within a fey court. The Hags have prophesied that the hostages will die if the alliance between the Houses is broken.

Rescuing the hostages or defeating the Oracle Hags would remove the threat maintaining the alliance.

It has been discovered that the lord is being advised by the [[Aly-an-ta]]. The Aly-an-ta have secretly taken control of the mine.
