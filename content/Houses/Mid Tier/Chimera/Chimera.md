# Chimera

**Formal family name:** House Trivayne  
**Slang name:** the Threeborn

House Chimera is allied with [[Owlbear|House Owlbear]]. It rules the city of [[Ferndale]] and operates a mine of Charm magic within the Castle of Ferndale.

It has been discovered that the lord is being advised by the [[Aly-an-ta]]. The Aly-an-ta have secretly taken control of the mine.
