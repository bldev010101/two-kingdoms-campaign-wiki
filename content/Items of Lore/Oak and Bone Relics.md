# Oak and Bone Relics

Oak and Bone Relics are made of brass and depict a huge oak tree canopy with roots made of bones. Each relic contains three slots for gems.

One completed relic was used to open the temple found underneath the sewers of [[Purple Worm|House Purple Worm]], near the House Purple Worm mines that produce Lightning magic. This relic has three green gems, each with the same rune shape carved within it.

One gem seemed to light up near a central altar in a dungeon that seemed to contain a map with three gem slots and the markings of a major temple in the middle. This major temple seemed to be the same temple found near the sewers.
