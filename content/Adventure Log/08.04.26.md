# 08.04.26

The players recovered the cursed [[Crown of Silence]] from some Ogres who had crawled out of dark cracks. The ancient crown was created by the elves of this land to fight against the "civilizers" of the land: the [[Ballado]] and [[Lycaria|Lycrian]] houses.

The altar tied to the [[Oak and Bone Relics|Oak and Bone Relic]] was studied further, revealing that three auxiliary temples seemed to unlock a main temple. The map suggested that the main temple depicted on the altar was the one beneath [[Purple Worm|House Purple Worm]] and its mines.

[[Thad]] tried to trap a huge Dire Bear but slipped and fell into the river.
