# 07.29.26

[[Princess Minerva Ballado|Minerva]] was informed that the [[Aly-an-ta]] were working behind the scenes to influence and give ill advice to members of certain [[Houses|houses]]. She wants [[Sylas]] to determine how widespread the Aly-an-ta influence is.

Minerva mentioned that [[Hydra|House Hydra]] had been acting strangely recently. She also said that the major city of [[Osperath]] was becoming a powder keg as many House garrisons were being moved there.

Minerva was secretly worried about the current strength of [[Tarrasque|House Tarrasque]]'s allegiance. If that allegiance fell, it could mean the end of [[Ballado]] rule.

An altar was found that seemed to be magically tied to the [[Oak and Bone Relics|Oak and Bone Relic]].

Some Ents infested with vines spoke of the [[Old Spirit]], which ruled the natural order of things, uncomplicated by the strange thoughts of the races.
