# Princess Minerva Ballado

Princess Minerva Ballado is concerned about changes in [[Factions|faction]] alliances. Her main concern is keeping the [[Ballado]] family in power in their kingdom.

Minerva was informed that the [[Aly-an-ta]] were working behind the scenes to influence and give ill advice to members of certain [[Houses|houses]]. She wants [[Sylas]] to determine how widespread their influence is.

She mentioned that [[Hydra|House Hydra]] had been acting strangely recently and that the major city of [[Osperath]] was becoming a powder keg as many House garrisons were being moved there.
