# Alliance Sheet

![[Alliance Web - Spy Report.png]]

This sheet tracks the alliances between the Houses and the changes that could prevent the conflict from spreading into a war between the two kingdoms.

## Starting Conflict

**Conflict:** [[Roc|House Roc]] versus [[Sphinx|House Sphinx]]

**Status:** The two Houses are already at war. This conflict cannot be prevented.

## House Sphinx Alliance Branch

House Sphinx is allied with [[Displacer Beast|House Displacer Beast]], [[Chimera|House Chimera]], and [[Phoenix|House Phoenix]].

**House Sphinx decision-maker:** High Councillor Ilyra Veyrath chairs the House's ruling council.

### House Displacer Beast

House Displacer Beast is a Top Tier House that has fallen on hard times.

**Current alliance:** House Sphinx

**Reason for the alliance:** House Displacer Beast lost access to its mines long ago. Its alliance with House Sphinx is helping it remain afloat and retain its Top Tier position.

**Who is determined to preserve the alliance:** Lord Vyaharian is too old to realize that his House is falling and would never consider switching kingdoms.

**Who would prefer a different course:** Loeket, Vyaharian's son, believes that switching kingdoms would be better.

**Possible change:** If House Displacer Beast could find an ally in [[Ballado]], it might be able to preserve its position without the support of House Sphinx and could break the alliance.

**Mid Tier ally:** [[Bulette|House Bulette]]

House Bulette is led by Guildmistress Daska Durnholt. It functions more like a thieves' guild and is known for underhanded black-market dealings. It has helped House Displacer Beast remain afloat through black-market deals.

House Bulette now sees that House Displacer Beast is falling from its position and is looking for another partner.

**Possible change to the Bulette alliance:** House Bulette would leave if it found another House willing to participate in its underhanded and thieving activities.

### House Chimera

House Chimera is a Mid Tier House whose fortunes are improving.

**Decision-maker:** Lord Cassian Trivayne

**Current alliance:** House Sphinx

**Reason for the alliance:** Elowen Trivayne of House Chimera and Tavian Veyrath of House Sphinx are held hostage by the Oracle Hags Mora, Cethra, and Veska within a fey court. The Hags have prophesied that the hostages will die if the alliance between the Houses is broken.

**Goal:** House Chimera intends to displace House Displacer Beast.

**Mid Tier ally:** [[Owlbear|House Owlbear]]

**Possible change to the Sphinx alliance:** Rescuing the fey hostages or defeating the Oracle Hags would remove the threat maintaining the alliance.

Speaker Tamsin Brackenmere represents House Owlbear's wandering council. House Owlbear is already upset with House Chimera because Chimera withdrew the garrison that protected it.

**Possible change to the Owlbear alliance:** House Owlbear would ally with another House if that House assigned a garrison to protect it.

### House Phoenix

House Phoenix does not have the same economic power as some other Top Tier Houses, but it has consistently maintained substantial political power within the [[Symposium]]. Lord Caelian Aurelian, Lady Mirelle Aurelian, and Lord Oren Aurelian currently hold three seats on the 12-seat Symposium, giving the House a larger share than most other Houses.

**Current alliance:** House Sphinx

**Reason for the alliance:** House Phoenix relies on House Sphinx's deep understanding of the religious aspects of the kingdoms to help maintain its political power. House Phoenix is skilled in diplomacy but does not have the same expertise in religion.

House Sphinx's lands contain the Tridecagon. There are thirteen major religions in the kingdoms, and although they do not often interact, rumors suggest that their religious heads meet within the Tridecagon for an unknown purpose.

**Possible change:** The alliance could be weakened if the players provide House Phoenix with another source of religious knowledge or make House Sphinx's religious expertise less important to Phoenix's politicians.

**Mid Tier ally:** [[Umber Hulk|House Umber Hulk]]

House Umber Hulk is governed by the Deep Council, led by First Councillor Hadrik Karthum. An aboleth named Ulu-Vor beneath the House's castle influences the council through dreams and maintains its alliance with House Phoenix.

**Possible change to the Umber Hulk alliance:** Killing the aboleth or exposing its influence would allow House Umber Hulk to reconsider the alliance.

## Lycaria's Final Domino

### House Purple Worm

House Purple Worm is the last domino to fall on the Lycarian side. Unlike [[Tarrasque|House Tarrasque]], this is not because it represents the kingdom's main ground army. House Purple Worm is significant because it is among the Houses least likely to want war.

**Decision-maker:** Mine-Matriarch Nara Odaku

House Purple Worm has no ties to the strange events and changes occurring within the kingdoms. It already holds a strong economic position through its elemental mines, which the kingdoms rely on for elemental magic. It has more to lose if war damages the kingdoms' economic stability.

The players are already aligned with House Purple Worm.

**Preferred alternative:** If dangerous alliances must be shifted, moving them toward House Purple Worm could create a more stable situation. House Purple Worm has strong economic reasons to preserve the status quo.

**Final domino:** If House Purple Worm is pulled into the war, even the House with the strongest reasons to preserve economic stability has joined the conflict.

## House Roc Alliance Branch

House Roc is allied with [[Gorgon|House Gorgon]] and [[Hydra|House Hydra]].

**House Roc decision-maker:** Lord Aeric Kyclyan

### House Gorgon

**Current alliance:** House Roc

**Decision-maker:** Lord Damar Dravosk

**Role in the alliance branch:** Auxiliary

**Reason for the alliance:** Seraphine Dravosk, daughter of Lord Damar Dravosk, is betrothed to Corven Kyclyan, son of Lord Aeric Kyclyan of House Roc.

**Possible change:** Seraphine is in love with Prince Lucan Malrec of [[Manticore|House Manticore]]. If the players help with that relationship, they could change the Gorgon-Roc alliance.

House Gorgon is also allied with [[Tarrasque|House Tarrasque]].

**Mid Tier ally:** House Manticore

House Manticore is led by Lady Ysilde Malrec. It possesses an Ancestral War Helm containing the spirit of General Corveth Malrec, an ancestor who compels the House to honor its alliance with House Gorgon.

**Possible change to the Manticore alliance:** Defeating the ancestral spirit or destroying the War Helm would break its control over the alliance.

**Reason for the Tarrasque alliance:** The alliance is maintained by four elder patriarchs: Garran and Othric Dravosk of House Gorgon, and Merek and Torvan Morcant of House Tarrasque. They have known one another for a very long time and remain closely tied. The rest of both families dislike them, regard them as strange, and suspect that they suffer from dementia. They also hold strange opinions.

**Possible change to the Tarrasque alliance:** Removing the four patriarchs from influence would break the alliance. The players could kill them, place them in care, or send them away somewhere remote.

### House Hydra

**Current alliance:** House Roc

**Decision-maker:** Lady Ysara Kaelroth

**Reason for the alliance:** House Roc purchases a large amount of House Hydra's healing magic. House Roc provides a market for the healing magic throughout the land and uses its airships and air cavalry to deliver House Hydra's goods.

**Possible change:** If the players find another way to deliver House Hydra's goods, they could change the Hydra-Roc alliance.

House Hydra is also allied with [[Tarrasque|House Tarrasque]].

**Reason for the Tarrasque alliance:** Lady Ysara Kaelroth holds substantial blackmail against Marshal-Lord Edric Morcant and Chancellor Beren Morcant of House Tarrasque.

House Hydra is already ostracized and viewed as strange.

**Possible change to the Tarrasque alliance:** The alliance could be broken if the players remove or stop the blackmail, or push House Hydra out completely by making it fully disreputable.

**Mid Tier ally:** [[Basilisk|House Basilisk]]

Lady Sabine Serevan, wife of Lord Orsian Serevan, is being slowly turned to stone by a cursed Petrifying Eye. House Hydra's healing magic slows the petrification, maintaining the alliance between the Houses.

**Possible change to the Basilisk alliance:** Destroying the Petrifying Eye or finding another cure would remove House Basilisk's dependence on House Hydra.

### House Nightmare

House Nightmare is a Mid Tier House that supplies horseback cavalry for [[Ballado]], while House Roc supplies air cavalry.

**Decision-maker:** Ranger-Marshal Maelin Vaelmoor

**Current alliance:** House Roc

**Reason for the alliance:** House Nightmare relies on House Roc's air cavalry.

House Nightmare does not want to remain aligned with House Roc. Its members are good people, and House Roc has been acting strangely.

**Possible change:** If House Nightmare gains effective anti-air magic, such as fireball or flame magic, it would no longer depend as heavily on House Roc. House Nightmare has been working with [[Owlbear|House Owlbear]], which may have magic that could help.

### House Wyvern

House Wyvern is a Mid Tier House allied directly with House Roc.

**Current alliance:** House Roc

Queen Velyra Vaskyr, the widowed queen of House Wyvern, would rather be allied with [[Gorgon|House Gorgon]] because she is in love with Halvek Dravosk, one of Lord Damar Dravosk's sons.

**Who is determined to preserve the alliance:** Chancellor Orren Vale, House Wyvern's main political adviser, has been seduced by the succubus Velisara and refuses to allow the queen to switch alliances.

**Possible change:** Removing the succubus's influence over the political adviser could allow the queen to move House Wyvern's alliance from House Roc to House Gorgon.

### House Tarrasque

**Current alliances:** House Gorgon and House Hydra

**Decision-maker:** Marshal-Lord Edric Morcant

House Tarrasque represents the main army of [[Ballado]]. If House Tarrasque enters the war, the conflict becomes a kingdom-wide war between Ballado and [[Lycaria]]. House Tarrasque is therefore the last domino to fall.

House Hydra maintains its alliance with House Tarrasque through blackmail against some of House Tarrasque's top lords. Removing that blackmail could break the alliance.

House Gorgon's alliance with House Tarrasque is maintained by Garran and Othric Dravosk of House Gorgon, and Merek and Torvan Morcant of House Tarrasque. Removing them from influence could break that alliance.
