# Inexplicable Machinations of the Inner Soul

## Sister Betheal's teachings

The teachings of [[Sister Betheal]], an acolyte of the religion, have risen in prominence. She teaches that members should learn about and practice new methods for trying to understand the hidden workings of the [[Soul]] and the universe contained within it. Members of the religion seem focused on this approach.

## Older teachings

Some of the older teachings and practices focused more on the Machinations: the plan for this reality that the [[Soul|Inner Soul]] has from within the [[Vessel|vessel]] it embodies. These teachings were concerned with the plan itself, rather than understanding why the Inner Soul desires that plan.
