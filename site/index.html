Campaign Synopsis

Eladrin forced out at the end of the Primal Age have returned to bring an end to the Age of the Races. Can our party of heroes use their unique abilites and connections to shape the world of the coming New Age?

Primal Age

The Primal Gods channeled the Divine Energry to create a dense ecosystem ruled by Natural Order. The Alyanta built Six Temples to further channel their Primal Divinity into these lands. Through these temples the Avatars of the Primal Gods were brought forth to walk and to shape the land.

The End of the Primal Age

As the Races spread across the land, the Alyanta's natural ways proved no match for the Race's complex needs to conquer and control. The Alyanta retreated into the realms of the Fey and Shadow, becoming known as Eladrin and Shadar-kai.

Age of the Races

The Lycerian and Ballado kingdoms were established. The Houses grew rich by mining out the magical viens of Residuum created by the hidden Primal Temples below the mountains. They cut down the trees and found the land fertile for farming.

The Races' spritual beliefs concentrated on the complex nature of their conscienceness and realities. This could not have been further from the worship of the Primal Gods. Thus Divinity no longer graced the Primal Gods, and the Gods of the Races now channeled the Divinity of these lands.

Return of the Alyanta

Now it seems the Alyanta have returned - the Eladrin long ago banished to the Fey realm and the Shadar-kai long ago banished to the Shadow Realm. Quietly they gathered new recruits from the Race of Elves. Small in number, they saw their best chance lay in inflitrating the ranks of the Houses of the Kingdom, to sow chaos and weakness.

The Plan: Destroy the Houses. Bring Down the Kingdoms

The Alyanta infiltrated positions of power promising great rewards. They ill-advised the Lords towards chaos.

With control of the Mines the Alyanta were able to send the Residumm back into the Temples to awaken and empower the Avatars of the Primal Gods.

Garrisons moved away from their posts to the impending war have allowed Beasts to ravage the land, causing refugees and starvation

Once true war breaks out between all of the houses of both kingdoms, only a weakened force will remain in the aftermath to oppose the Alyanta, their beasts, and their Avatars.

The war was set off by Sphinx and Roc. Had some dark promise of immortality been made to those Lords as well?

The Plan: Destroy the Modern Gods. Bring Back the Primal Gods

The Alyanta developed substances to weaken the minds of the 13 major Modern Religions.

The weaker the Modern Religions, the stronger the Avatars could become. Each Church that falls out of the protection of their weakend Modern God becomes an easy target for an Avatar.

The Coming of the New Age

Push the Houses to total war. Restore the 6 Avatars to full power. Reclaim the land for the Alyanta and the Primal Gods.

The coming on of the New Age is impossible to stop. If the chips fall as they currently stand, the land will return to its Primal state, maintained by a small but devouted group of Alyatna and ruled over by the few immortal remanants of House Lords left to plunder the excesses of the land to purely their own benefit and pleasure.

But some Heroic few may be able to at least change the course of the changing of this Age into a third Age that aligns with their vision of a future they would like to see.

Q and As

What can I do to stop the Houses from going to total war?

Change the alliances of the Houses to stop the dominoes from falling.
or, Get the Garrisons back to where they belong and off of the frontlines.
or, Kill the Alyanta advisors and any corrupted House members (Especially the Lords of Sphinx and Roc).

What can I do to restore the power of the modern religions?

Stop the influx of mind-altering substances and restore the proper teachings.
or, kill Letha and the other Alyanta taken hold of the Tridecagon.

What can I do to stop the Avatars from being summoned at full strength?

Gain access to the remain temples (Water, Ground, and Forest) and concegrate them fully to a modern God.
or, take control of the mines feeding Residumm to the Temples (Charm, Healing, and Elemental).

What can I do to help House Worg become more powerful?

Rise in glory during the Festival of Blood (Blood Sport tournament).
or, Find a source of strong revenue like a Mine.
or, Create a strong Alliance through a marriage (Michaelango is currently married to an unimporant woman. Beathrice is a widow).

What can I do to restore the Garrisons?

Kill any beasts that have taken residence in the garrison. Get the commanding Lord to allow the Garrison to return to where they are supposed to be - intimidate, assassinate, or de-esculate the threat of that House going to war

What can I do to assist the Humble Knights in aiding the downtroden?

Restore their villages ravenged by beasts.
or, get them money from the Houses by some means.
or, find out way so many are going missing.
or, figure out the ways the House Lords are keeping the populace compliant.
